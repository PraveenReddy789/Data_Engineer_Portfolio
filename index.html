About me

I'm a data engineer at Verbalyze, an AI voice-bot analytics platform for BFSI debt collection. I work on the part most people don't see: getting call data in, cleaning it, structuring it with LLMs and making sure the outputs are trustworthy enough for audits and business decisions.

I like work where the data has to be right. That means schemas that hold up under load, pipelines that recover when something fails, privacy controls built in from the start, and interfaces that let analysts ask meaningful questions without wrestling with raw data.
